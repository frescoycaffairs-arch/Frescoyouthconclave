Principle & Purpose

Redefining Diplomatic Simulation for a Fractured World Order

The 21st-century international architecture stands at an inflection point. The postwar multilateral consensus is strained under currency fragmentation, autonomous conflict enclaves, and asymmetric cyber warfare. The Fresco Youth Conclave was established with a singular conviction: that the next generation of sovereign leaders cannot be trained on theatrical platitudes.

Set against the diplomatic grandeur of Chanakyapuri, New Delhi, FYC unites scholars, debaters, and prospective policymakers from across 28 Indian states and international delegations. Over two days of intensive parliamentary debate, delegates will deconstruct complex geopolitical fault lines, draft binding resolutions, and confront unscheduled crisis flashpoints.

Protocol & Etiquette

Strict Diplomatic Sartorial Standard

Gentlemen: Tailored charcoal, midnight navy, or black two-piece suits; pressed white or pale blue Oxford dress shirts; conservative silk neckties (half or full Windsor knot); leather Oxford brogues or monk straps. Pocket squares must be understated.
Ladies: Structured pant-suits, tailored blazer with knee-length formal pencil skirt or sheath dress; formal button-up shirts or conservative silk blouses; formal closed-toe pumps or dress flats.
Indian Formal Attire: High-collar Bandhgalas, Achkans, formal Kurta-Pyjama with structured Nehru jackets, or formal Kanjeevaram / Chanderi silk sarees paired with a structured blazer.
International Diplomatic Delegations: Formal national diplomatic attire representing the cultural heritage of the delegate’s assigned sovereign state (e.g., Barong Tagalog, Hanbok, Thobe with Bisht, Agbada).

Frequently Asked Questions

Delegate registration provides comprehensive access to all conclave proceedings, including:
  • Full entry to all six specialized committee sessions over two full days
  • Personalized diplomatic acrylic country placard and executive folder
  • Premium executive conference stationery, notepad, pen, and official lanyard badge
  • Two days of gourmet five-star buffet lunches and morning/afternoon high-tea catered by New Delhi Conference Venue
  • Exclusive entry to the Ambassadorial Diplomatic Soirée & High Tea
  • Official certified participation credential signed by the Secretary-General
  • Full eligibility for Best Delegate gavels, merit citations, and delegation awards.

Country allocations are administered by the FYC Secretariat via a multi-factor merit formula:
  • Submission Priority (20%): Early applications receive preferential review.
  • Diplomatic Experience (45%): Evaluation of past conferences attended, committees chaired, and awards won.
  • Policy Essay & Rationale (35%): Quality and legal depth of the delegate’s portfolio justification submitted during registration.
Allotments are communicated via email within 72 hours of complete application verification.

Double delegations are permitted strictly in two committees characterized by high-velocity multilateral negotiations:
  • United Nations Security Council (UNSC): To balance Chapter VII legal drafting with fast-paced real-time crisis resolution.
  • BRICS Summit: To divide responsibilities between sovereign political positions and technical central bank local-currency settlement mechanics.
All other councils (OIC-CFM, ICPO-INTERPOL, UNHRC, and UN Women) are strictly single-delegate forums to ensure individual oratorical and legal accountability.

Submission of an authoritative Position Paper is mandatory for any delegate seeking consideration for Best Delegate, High Commendation, or Special Mention honors:
  • Format: Maximum of two A4 pages (single-spaced, 11pt Georgia or Times New Roman, 1-inch margins).
  • Content Requirements: (1) Sovereign background and past international actions, (2) Critical appraisal of existing multilateral treaties, and (3) Concrete, innovative operative proposals with identified funding sources.
  • Deadline: All position papers must be uploaded via the delegate portal by April 07, 2027 at 23:59 IST. Papers are audited through Turnitin with a strict <10% similarity threshold.

Awards and sovereign citations are adjudicated by the Executive Board under rigorous academic criteria:
  • Best Delegate (6 Committees): Hand-crafted solid brass gavel trophy + Official Commendation Letter.
  • High Commendation (6 Committees): Silver medallion + Official Certificate of Distinction.
  • Special Mention (6 Committees): Bronze medallion + Official Certificate of Merit.
  • Best Delegation Trophy (Institutional): Awarded to the school, university, or independent delegation demonstrating highest aggregate score across councils.
